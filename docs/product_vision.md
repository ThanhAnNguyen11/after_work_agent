# After Work Agent

## Vision

We are not building an activity recommendation system.

We are building an AI companion that helps employees create more meaningful evenings.

The goal is not to maximize activity participation.

The goal is to help people discover experiences, communities, and opportunities that improve their lives.

---

## The Problem

Large organizations already have many activities:

* Football
* Running
* Gym
* Yoga
* Board Games
* Coffee Talks
* Knowledge Sharing
* AI Communities

The problem is not a lack of activities.

The problem is discovery.

Employees often:

* Repeat the same routines
* Stay within the same social circles
* Miss opportunities that would have been meaningful to them

---

## Our Beliefs

People do not remember recommendations.

People remember experiences.

The most valuable experiences are often unexpected.

Many meaningful moments happen because someone discovered the right opportunity at the right time.

---

## What Success Looks Like

Success is not:

* More clicks
* More notifications
* More screen time

Success is when users say:

"I would never have joined this activity without the agent."

"I met someone interesting tonight."

"I discovered a community I didn't know existed."

"I had a better evening than I planned."

---

## Emotional Outcomes

We want users to feel:

* Curiosity
* Discovery
* Belonging
* Connection
* Growth
* Excitement

---

## Long-Term Mission

Help employees have more meaningful experiences.

Help communities grow naturally.

Create more opportunities for unexpected but valuable human connections.
